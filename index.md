# About Me
 <div class="row">
  <div class="column" markdown="1">
I am a Staff Human Vision Scientist at Magic Leap on the Quantified Experience team working on studying visual perception to improve product design for augmented reality wearables. Previously, I was a Senior Vision Scientist at J&J Vision Care on the lens design team. I completed my Ph.D. in psychology at the University of Pennsylvania, where I was co-advised by David Brainard and Geoffrey Aguirre. My research interests are in using psychophysics, computational modeling, and neuroimaging to study color perception and, more broadly, visual processing.

Before grad school, I was a lab manager for Kalanit Grill-Spector at Stanford University. During this time, I examined both the development of high-level vision and the microarchitecture of functional regions in the human cortex. Prior to being a lab manager, I was a research assistant for Emily Grossman at UC Irvine, where I worked on understanding the neural representation of biological motion.
